## Selection notes

The ten-round set combines seven birds with three marsupials. Koala uses the clearly labelled CC0 field recording originally suggested in the brief. The Tasmanian devil cry and possum feeding recording are CC BY 4.0. The possum stimulus is audible apple crunching rather than a vocal call and is identified as such in the reveal. Quokka was not included: an authoritative zoo source notes that the literature does not describe its vocalisations, and no distinctive, reusable recording was verified.
